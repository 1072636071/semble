# 商业头像 / 职业形象照

目标是「像真人、能用、不惹眼」：干净背景 + 柔光 + 中性表情 + 肩线水平。

## 骨架

[身份与年龄], [表情], [着装], [姿态与肩线], [背景], [光位], [镜头与裁切]

## 模块词库

| 模块 | 关键词示例 |
| --- | --- |
| 身份年龄 | 30s professional woman, product manager, approachable |
| 表情 | relaxed half smile, eyes open, neutral pleasant |
| 着装 | navy blazer over white shirt, no loud pattern |
| 姿态肩线 | shoulders squared to camera, slight body turn, upright posture |
| 背景 | soft grey seamless, blurred office可选 |
| 光位 | large softbox 45 degrees, fill card, soft shadow under chin |
| 镜头裁切 | 85mm f/2.8, head-and-shoulders crop, eye-level |

## 完整示例

职业形象照，30 多岁女性产品经理，亲和得体，放松的浅笑，眼睛睁开有神，穿藏青色西装外套配白衬衫，无夸张图案，双肩正对镜头、身体略侧，姿态挺拔，柔和灰色无缝背景，45 度大柔光箱加反光板补光，下颌有柔和阴影，85mm f/2.8，头肩构图，平视。

## 英文版示例

professional headshot, woman in her early thirties, product manager, approachable and composed, relaxed half smile, alert eyes, navy blazer over a white shirt with no loud pattern, shoulders squared to camera with a slight body turn, upright posture, soft grey seamless backdrop, large softbox at 45 degrees with fill card and a soft shadow under the chin, 85mm f/2.8, head-and-shoulders crop, eye-level camera

## 负面提示词

harsh shadow, wide-angle distortion, cropped head, heavy makeup, busy background, logo, text, watermark

## 调参备注

- 想当头像用：`--ar 1:1`（MJ）或 size 方图；头肩构图别裁到下巴。
- 表情写「放松浅笑」；写「大笑」会崩牙，写「严肃」会像证件照。
- 一稿多能用：换西装颜色 / 换背景灰阶即得同人多版本。
