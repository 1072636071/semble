# Keyword Research — Detailed Instructions

Keyword Research 技能的紧凑工作流、评分规则与交付模板。

每阶段开始时公告 `**[Phase X/8: Name]**`。

## Phase 1/8: Scope

确认 topic、受众、业务目标、domain strength、地域与语言。

## Phase 2/8: Discover

从以下播种：
- 核心产品与服务词
- 问题导向关键词
- 解决方案导向关键词
- 受众专属语言
- 行业术语

## Phase 3/8: Variations

```markdown
## Keyword Expansion Patterns

### Modifiers
- Best [keyword]
- Top [keyword]
- [keyword] for [audience]
- [keyword] [year]
- How to [keyword]
- What is [keyword]
- [keyword] vs [alternative]
- [keyword] examples
- [keyword] tools

### Long-tail Variations
- [keyword] for beginners
- [keyword] for small business
- Free [keyword]
- [keyword] software/tool/service
- [keyword] template
- [keyword] checklist
- [keyword] guide
```

## Phase 4/8: Classify

| Intent | Signals | Example | Content Type |
|--------|---------|---------|--------------|
| Informational | what、how、why、guide、learn | "what is SEO" | Guide、explainer |
| Navigational | brand name、特定站点 | "google analytics login" | 首页、support |
| Commercial | best、review、vs、compare | "best SEO tools" | Comparison、review |
| Transactional | buy、price、discount、order | "buy SEO software" | 产品与 pricing 页 |

## Phase 5/8: Score

Difficulty 分档：
- **Low (1-39)**：弱竞品、薄内容或 long-tail intent
- **Medium (40-69)**：混合权威格局、中等链接/内容门槛
- **High (70-100)**：大品牌、重 backlink 需求、ad 密集 SERP

Opportunity 公式：

`Opportunity = (Volume × Intent Value) / Difficulty`

Intent value：
- Informational = 1
- Navigational = 1
- Commercial = 2
- Transactional = 3

| Scenario | Volume | Difficulty | Intent | Priority |
|----------|--------|------------|--------|----------|
| Quick Win | Low-Med | Low | High | 5 stars |
| Growth | High | Medium | High | 4 stars |
| Long-term | High | High | High | 3 stars |
| Research | Low | Low | Low | 2 stars |

## Phase 6/8: GEO-Check

标记高 AI 回答重叠的关键词：
- 问题、定义、对比、列表与 how-to 查询
- 可简洁回答或用结构化事实回答的查询
- 文档完善且商业摩擦低到中的 topic

## Phase 7/8: Cluster

```markdown
## Topic Cluster: [Main Topic]

**Pillar Content**: [Primary keyword]
- Search volume: [X]
- Difficulty: [X]
- Content type: [Guide / landing / comparison]

**Cluster Content**:
- [Secondary keyword] — Volume [X], Difficulty [X], links to [pillar]
- [Secondary keyword] — Volume [X], Difficulty [X], links to [pillar + related cluster]
```

## Phase 8/8: Deliver

报告章节：
- Executive Summary
- Top Opportunities: Quick Wins / Growth / GEO
- Topic Clusters
- Content Calendar
- Next Steps

**Quality bar**：每条建议至少含一个具体数字。

| Generic | Actionable |
|---|---|
| "Target long-tail keywords for better results" | "Target 'project management for nonprofits' (vol: 320, KD: 22) — top 10 无 DR>40 站点" |
| "This keyword has good potential" | "Opportunity 8.4: vol 4,800, KD 28, transactional intent — top 5 内容陈旧" |
| "Consider creating content around this topic" | "Write '[Tool A] vs [Tool B] for small teams' — 1,200/月搜索，现任 #1 来自 2022" |

> **Reference**：完整报告模板与示例见 [references/example-report.md](example-report.md)。

## Advanced Usage

- **Intent Mapping**：`Map all keywords for [topic] by search intent and funnel stage`
- **Seasonal Analysis**：`Identify seasonal keyword trends for [industry]`
- **Competitor Gap**：`What keywords do [competitor 1], [competitor 2] rank for that I'm missing?`
- **Local Keywords**：`Research local keywords for [business type] in [city/region]`

## Tips for Success

从强 seed 出发、尊重 intent、紧凑聚类、纳入 GEO 候选、每季度刷新研究。
