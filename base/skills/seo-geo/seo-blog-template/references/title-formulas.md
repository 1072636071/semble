# Title and Headline Formulas

用这些 copy-start 模式写出匹配意图、赢得点击、并落在 SERP 显示限制内的标题。默认目标：**50-60 字符**；移动端为主的页面宜 **50-55 字符**。

## Formula Matrix

| Intent | Pattern | Best for | Example shape |
|--------|---------|----------|---------------|
| List | `[N] [Adjective] [Topic] [Benefit]` | Roundup、战术、工具 | `7 Proven SEO Tests for Faster Indexing` |
| How-to | `How to [Goal] in [Timeframe]` | 教程、工作流 | `How to Build Topic Clusters in 30 Days` |
| How-to with objection | `How to [Goal] Without [Pain]` | 障碍消除 | `How to Grow Traffic Without Paid Ads` |
| Beginner | `How to [Goal] (Even If [Limitation])` | 新受众 | `How to Rank Locally (Even with a New Site)` |
| Definition | `What Is [Topic]? [Benefit/Hook]` | 教育型查询 | `What Is Technical SEO? A Practical Guide` |
| Why | `Why [Problem/Action] [Result]` | 诊断、反共识角度 | `Why Most SEO Audits Miss Crawl Waste` |
| Decision | `Should You [Action]? [Quick Answer]` | 风险或购买问题 | `Should You Buy Backlinks? The Safer Answer` |
| Comparison | `[A] vs [B]: Which Is Better for [Use Case]?` | 商业调研 | `WordPress vs Webflow: Which Is Better for SEO?` |
| Alternatives | `[N] Best [Product] Alternatives in [Year]` | 工具切换 | `7 Best Ahrefs Alternatives in 2026` |
| Guide | `The [Complete/Definitive] Guide to [Topic]` | Pillar 内容 | `The Complete Guide to Technical SEO` |
| Audience guide | `[Topic]: The Ultimate Guide for [Audience]` | 分群页 | `Local SEO: The Ultimate Guide for Clinics` |
| Before/after | `From [Bad State] to [Good State]: [Proof/Method]` | 案例研究 | `From 0 to 10K Visits: The Content System` |
| Mistake | `[N] [Topic] Mistakes [Consequence]` | 纠错 | `5 SEO Mistakes Killing Product Pages` |
| Fix | `Why Your [Topic] Is Not Working (And How to Fix It)` | 排障 | `Why your Content Is Not Ranking (And How to Fix It)` |
| Checklist | `[Topic] Checklist: [N] Must-Check Items` | 审计、实施 | `Technical SEO Checklist: 23 Must-Check Items` |

## CTR Modifiers

| Modifier type | Use when | Examples |
|---------------|----------|----------|
| Freshness | 主题随时间变化 | `2026`, `Updated`, `Current` |
| Proof | 声明需要可信度 | `Tested`, `Proven`, `With Data` |
| Completeness | 页面是权威资源 | `Complete`, `Essential`, `Definitive` |
| Speed | 用户需要快速行动 | `Quick`, `Fast`, `Today` |
| Audience | SERP 有分群意图 | `for Beginners`, `for B2B`, `for SaaS` |
| Cost | 工具或购买意图 | `Free`, `Low-Cost`, `Budget` |

避免全大写、过度标点、无依据的最高级、含糊标题党，以及内容无法兑现的承诺。

## Length Guidelines

| Surface | Optimal | Truncation risk |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| Google desktop SERP | 50-60 chars | 约 70 chars |
| Google mobile SERP | 50-55 chars | 约 55 chars |
| Email subject | 40-50 chars | 约 60 chars |
| Facebook | 40-50 chars | 视上下文而定 |
| Twitter/X | 70-100 chars | 视上下文而定 |

## Rewrite Examples

| Weak title | Stronger title | Why it works |
|------------|----------------|--------------|
| `SEO Tips` | `How We Increased Organic Traffic by [X]% in [timeframe]` | 第一方证据 + 占位符 |
| `How to Do Keyword Research` | `How to Do Keyword Research with Free Tools` | 增加约束与价值 |
| `Content Marketing Guide` | `The Complete Content Marketing Guide for SMBs` | 明确范围与受众 |
| `WordPress vs Shopify` | `WordPress vs Shopify: Which Is Better for SEO?` | 增加决策框架 |

## Title QA

- [ ] 主关键词自然出现。
- [ ] 搜索意图与内容格式匹配 SERP。
- [ ] 标题具体、真实、可落实。
- [ ] 长度适配目标展示面。
- [ ] 修饰词带来真实价值，非标题党。
- [ ] 移动端截断后主承诺仍清晰。
- [ ] 标题与头部竞品有实质差异。
